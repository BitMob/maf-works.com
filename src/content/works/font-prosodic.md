/prəˈsɑːdɪk/ adj. 詩體學的、作詩法的、韻律的

在 2024 年為杭州[天目里美術館](https://www.byartmatters.com)設計的《Prosodic Notes 浮聲筆記》出版物中標題字體的基礎上，優化並延伸出以「Prosodic / 韻律的」為名的可變字體，單軸字重變化。

Art Director / Graphic Designer: maf-works.com<br/>

/prəˈsɑːdɪk/ adj. prosodic; relating to prosody, versification, or poetic metre

Based on the display typeface originally designed for the 2024 publication Prosodic Notes for [BY ART MATTERS](https://www.byartmatters.com), Hangzhou, the design was refined and expanded into a variable typeface named "Prosodic". It features a single weight axis.

Art Director / Graphic Designer: maf-works.com<br/>
