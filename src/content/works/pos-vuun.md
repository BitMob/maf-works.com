通過成雙出現的魚與彼此纏繞的紅掌花，表達人與人之間偶然相遇、持續連接與相互依存的關係。兩條魚以環繞、呼應的姿態形成流動感，像兩個人在各自軌跡中相遇並同行；紅掌的莖葉則彼此交織，將這種關係進一步延伸為一種不斷生長的連接。整體以紅、粉、黃與藍綠色構成溫暖而鮮明的色彩關係，並結合帶有印刷顆粒感的圖像與排版，讓生日聚會的親密、輕鬆氣氛與「緣」所包含的偶然性和牽引感同時存在。<br/>
Art Director / Graphic Designer: maf-works.com<br/>

Through the paired fish and intertwined anthurium flowers, the design expresses the chance encounters, lasting connections, and mutual dependence between people. The two fish circle and respond to one another, creating a sense of movement, like two people meeting along their own paths and continuing forward together. The intertwined stems and leaves of the anthuriums extend this relationship into a connection that keeps growing. A warm yet vivid palette of red, pink, yellow, and blue-green is combined with grainy, print-like imagery and typography, bringing together the intimate, relaxed atmosphere of a birthday gathering with the sense of chance and invisible pull contained in the idea of “缘” (fate or serendipitous connection).<br/>
Art Director / Graphic Designer: maf-works.com<br/>
