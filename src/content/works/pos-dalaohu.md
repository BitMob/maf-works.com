海報為 2026 年九月在廣州 GUM 的一个周末音樂活動而設計。「今晚打老虎」來自 1991 年電影《賭俠2之上海灘賭聖》，是法語「Comment allez-vous?」的中文空耳，原意是「您好嗎？」<br/>
Art Director / Graphic Designer: maf-works.com<br/>

<!-- lang -->

The poster was designed for a weekend music event held at GUM in Guangzhou in September 2026. "Tonight We Beat the Tiger" (今晚打老虎) comes from the 1991 film *God of Gamblers Part III: Back to Shanghai* (賭俠2之上海灘賭聖), and is a Chinese homophone for the French phrase "Comment allez-vous?", which means "How are you?"<br/>
Art Director / Graphic Designer: maf-works.com<br/>
