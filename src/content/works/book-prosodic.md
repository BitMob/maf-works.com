2025 年 3 月為杭州[天目里美術館](https://www.byartmatters.com)展覽「浮聲切響」設計了展覽海報和配套出版物《浮聲筆記》。出版物涵蓋本次參展的 21 組藝術家訪談及相關作品介紹。

設計概念圍繞普通話四聲調符號展開，將主標題的漢語拼音「轉譯」成四聲調符號圖形，並於跨頁空間內騰挪排列。語言的抑揚頓挫化成特有的「樂譜」。在設計過程中，嘗試以這種方式將漢字的音樂性轉移到印刷品上。

封面及書脊處「Prosodic Notes」所用的英文字體，是特別為本次展覽全新設計的字體包，以傾斜的橢圓、豎線等特徵模仿西洋現代音樂的五線譜符號。

書封使用 265 克雅特超感紙，無色壓印音調符號的外框；內頁使用 110 克雪松超感紙，帶來鬆軟、粗糙的翻閱手感，同時也造成顏色偏沉，以及紙粉脫落後露白的結果，但還好。

語言：中文、英文\
尺寸：185 × 260 ㎜\
頁數：287

In March 2025, I designed the exhibition poster and the accompanying publication *Notes on Floating Sounds* for "Prosodic Grains" at [BY ART MATTERS](https://www.byartmatters.com), Hangzhou. The publication features interviews with 21 participating artists and artist groups, together with introductions to their exhibited works.

The design concept centres on the four tone marks of Mandarin Chinese. The pinyin of the main title was "translated" into graphic forms derived from these tonal marks, which shift and rearrange themselves across the spreads. The rises and falls of spoken language become a distinctive kind of "musical score". Through this process, I attempted to transfer the musicality of Chinese characters onto the printed page.

The Latin typeface used for "Prosodic Notes" on the cover and spine was newly designed specifically for the exhibition. Its slanted ellipses, vertical strokes, and other formal characteristics imitate symbols found in modern Western musical notation.

The cover was printed on 265 gsm Arjowiggins Curious Matter paper, with the outlines of the tonal symbols blind embossed. The interior pages use 110 gsm Cedar uncoated paper, creating a soft and slightly rough tactile experience. This choice also resulted in somewhat subdued colours and occasional white specks caused by paper dust coming loose, but the overall effect remains acceptable.
